# About

I like problems with a clear verdict. At work that means services that stay up, designs that survive review and deploys that are boring. Off the clock it means a contest clock running down and one more test case to pass.

## What I do

I'm a backend-focused engineer with 5+ years at SELISE Digital Platforms in Dhaka. I'm a core engineer on EcoHub, an insurance platform used by top Swiss insurers. I design the backend services and lead the Angular client that uses them.

  • Distributed services in C# / .NET with CQRS, event-driven architecture and Domain-Driven Design
  • Design docs first: I validate the approach with UI/UX and the client before writing code
  • Production ownership: on-call for Swiss deployments, with Application Insights and Seq

Coaching Center Fee Manager
